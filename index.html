About

Spaces planned from the first sketch to the last drawing — cafés, stores and plants.

카페와 매장 인테리어, 베트남 하이퐁과 창원의 플랜트 건축, 송도 K1 Project, 평택&천안 롯데웰푸드까지 공간을 기획하고 도면으로 완성해 왔습니다. 추가적으로 공무를 맡으며 현장에서 바로 쓰는 작은 도구도 직접 만듭니다.

From café and retail interiors to plant architecture in Haiphong, Vietnam and Changwon, the Songdo K1 Project, and Lotte Wellfood’s Pyeongtaek and Cheonan plants, I have planned spaces and carried them through to finished drawings. Alongside construction management, I also build small tools that are put to use on site right away.
